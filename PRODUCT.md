# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web, desktop and mobile browsers; Chinese-language, click/touch-led guided television experience.

## Users
Viewers revisiting Twin Peaks seasons 1–2, including Lynch admirers who want a quiet nostalgic experience without movement controls. The opening explicitly warns of full spoilers and estimates 20–30 minutes; that is an editorial estimate, not measured completion time.

## Product Purpose
“ 双峰 · 最后一盘录像带 ” invites the viewer to observe, remember and collect ten moments from the first two seasons, ending with a tribute to David Lynch (1946–2025) and acknowledgment of the series’ other creators.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Ten authored tableaux, thirty observation beats, ten two-option reflection prompts, ten scene stickers and three optional author easter eggs: thirteen collectible stickers in total.
- Read all three observations and choose a reflection to unlock the next scene. Collecting a sticker is optional. The ten-stop tape supports revisiting unlocked scenes; collected stickers also return to their scenes. Choices record attention in the journal and never change canonical character fates.
- Mouse, touch, Tab and Enter operate controls. WASD and arrows do not move or navigate a character. Native dialogs support Escape; pause, mute, readable narrative and reduced-motion settings are available.
- Progress, unlocked scenes, observations, choices, collections, completion and preferences persist under localStorage key `twin-peaks-tv-cut-v1` with version 1. If storage fails, the deck reports memory-only progress. A confirmed restart clears the run but retains sound and motion preferences. There is no account or cloud synchronization.
- Ten Blender-authored miniature sets and eight stylized Twin Peaks character models render as actual Three.js geometry with PBR materials. Four camera-angle controls, named cast portrait buttons and projected observation targets guide inspection; incoming and outgoing sets move in world space. Each character has a basic 16-bone rig and a quiet head-observation loop, frozen in gentle mode. The eight characters now have differentiated portrait geometry, continuous hair silhouettes, corrected scene wardrobe and material-batched meshes. They remain stylized interpretations, without actor scans, facial animation, lip sync, walking or full scene acting. Blender procedural material bump does not transfer to the GLB runtime; no photographic image plates are used. Audio is original Web Audio synthesis, not the series soundtrack or actor imitation.
- This is an unofficial selected-scene adaptation, not all thirty episodes, a full script reproduction, an official sequel, or a branching replacement ending.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the incumbent serif, pine, velvet, coffee warmth and cream-paper vocabulary. Let images and quiet narrative lead; amber transport and a paper sticker album give the viewer clear actions. Keep violence off-screen. Honor Lynch alongside Mark Frost, Angelo Badalamenti and the performers and production team.

## Product Principles
Scene first; one deliberate observation at a time. Text remains available without audio, hover or character movement. Choices preserve the viewer’s perspective, not alternate canonical outcomes. Separate sourced facts, Chinese paraphrase, visual interpretation and explicitly labeled cross-episode montage. Report inaccessible sources honestly.

## Open Decisions
No unresolved product decision blocks this cut. Broader episode coverage, finished facial/scene acting, new authored endings and cloud saves are outside this milestone. Font additions must extend the licensed display subset if new headings use uncovered characters.

## Legacy Context
Earlier iterations used `src/twinPeaksMain.js`, Three.js roaming, four environments, a miniature-town navigator, relation boards and a separate life archive. Their remaining code, procedural assets and portraits are legacy material, not the current entry point or current acceptance criteria. `docs/redesign/` and older design-token records concern those iterations. Their retained visual contribution is the serif/pine/velvet/paper vocabulary; legacy movement and geometry claims must not be applied to the current miniature cut. The former AI scene art is retained as historical concept material and is not loaded as current scenery.
